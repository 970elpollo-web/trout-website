The Vibe

The Patio. Beach rules apply.

Delta is high desert, dusty and bright. So we hung surfboards on the walls and put misters over the patio. Ten steps off Main Street and it feels like a beach day, minus the sand in your sandwich.

Nobody has surfed the Gunnison on a longboard yet. We keep one waxed just in case. Until then the boards stay bolted to the wall, the misters stay on, and the sunset over the Grand Mesa does the rest of the decorating for free.

Dogs welcome on the patio. Flip flops encouraged, never required.

The Kitchen Outside

El Pollo.

Yofran's wife is from Colombia, and the family recipes ride along in the truck parked out front. The chicken cooks over real charcoal. The green sauce goes on everything (someone put it on a margarita rim once, and honestly we let it happen).

Order at the window or at the bar. Either way the food finds your table on the patio. It always does.
